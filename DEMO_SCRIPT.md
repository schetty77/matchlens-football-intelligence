| Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Dashboard landing view | “MatchLens turns live synthetic football events into explainable turning points for both casual fans and analysts.” |

| 1:08–1:28 | Decisive-goal card and evidence links | “The winner rule checks that the goal broke a draw and that the opposition never scored afterward in the stream.” |
| 1:28–1:43 | Event table and raw data | “The whole pipeline is inspectable: synthetic events in, deterministic rules, then evidence-linked on-screen explanations.” |
| 1:43–1:55 | Return to cards | “MatchLens makes real-time football intelligence explainable, tailored and safe to verify.” |

- Run `python verify_demo.py` and capture the PASS line if desired.
- Run the three commands in `README.md`, then open the displayed local URL.
- Use a clean browser window at a readable zoom level.
- Keep the recording under two minutes and in English.
- Do not show passwords, emails, personal tabs, licensed match footage, music or third-party trademarks.
